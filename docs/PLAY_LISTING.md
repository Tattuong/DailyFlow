## Short description

## Full description
DailyFlow is a schedule of routines, not a row of checkmarks. Build a morning, a tidy, or a bedtime chain. Each step has a duration and a short cue.

Run them in order. Pause, skip, or add a minute. Weekdays and weekends can use different plans. Progress shows what you finished and which step you tend to leave unfinished.

Three routines are included. Add your own on this phone. No account.

Looks, keep-screen-on, tap sound, and DailyFlow Pro (more than 8 routines you create) unlock with coins from the daily reward or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and DailyFlow Pro are one-time unlocks.
