Privacy Policy – DailyFlow

Last updated: October 2026

DailyFlow is a private, on-device guided routine. Your routines, sessions, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account or upload routine text.

Data stored on device

  • Routines you create, step notes, and session logs
  • Coins, owned shop items, and the active look
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and DailyFlow Pro

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and DailyFlow Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N304.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

We do not sell personal data. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
